How the coinflip demo works

Coinflip is one of the simplest casino-style games: you pick a side, stake an amount and let the coin spin. In real lobbies, it’s usually presented with custom skins, graphics or multipliers. Here we focus on the basic 50/50 version, with a clean 3D animation and instant results.

Each flip in this demo is independent and randomly selects either Heads or Tails with equal probability. Wins pay 1:1: if you bet $10 and win, you receive $20 back (your stake plus $10 profit). If you lose, the stake is deducted from your virtual balance.

Controls in this simulator

  • Side selection: choose Heads or Tails before the flip.
  • Chip size: 1, 5, 10 or 25 units.
  • Bet amount: adjust using +/– or Clear.
  • Flip coin: starts the animation and locks the bet.

The stats panel tracks your total wagered amount, profit, sample RTP, number of flips, win rate and best streak in the current session.

Using coinflip as a variance and discipline tool

Because coinflip is conceptually simple, it’s great for showing how swingy even “balanced” games can be. Long streaks of Heads or Tails happen more often than most people expect, and this demo makes that visible without any risk.

Ideas for practical use

  • Simulate 50–100 flips and track how often long losing streaks appear.
  • Test different bet sizes and see how aggressive pacing affects your bankroll over time.
  • Compare flat betting with “chasing losses” approaches — and see why the latter can be dangerous.
